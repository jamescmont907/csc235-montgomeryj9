## Why I added my Components

### Carousel

I chose to do a carousel because I like adding images to my webpages, as it adds an interesting thing to look at, but they often
take up too much space and making them smaller effects the viewablity, and the carousel was the perfect solution to still having multiple images, but without them taking up the whole page, as you can flip between them

### Accordion

I chose an accordion for a similar to reason to why I picked a carousel, because it allows for more infomation, text in this case to be displayed on the page without taking up so much space like having 3 paragraphs would. It also allows me to display information that may not be as important to show and only can be viewed if the person is interested, and they reminded me of the Google question and answers you see when you search
